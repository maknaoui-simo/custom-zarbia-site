Privacy policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Who we are

Custom Zarbia is a private internal application used only by the Custom Zarbia team. Contact: dev.awtof@gmail.com.

Google user data we access

The application requests a single Google permission: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file. With it, the application can only see and manage files that it created itself in the connected Google Drive. It cannot read, list or change any other file in that Drive.

How we use it

  • We store encrypted backup copies of the company's own work photos (rug and delivery photos) in a dedicated folder of the company's own Google Drive account.
  • Files are encrypted on our server before upload. Google Drive only holds encrypted data, named with random identifiers.
  • We read those files back only to show them to authorized team members inside the application.

Sharing

We do not sell, share or transfer Google user data to anyone. We do not use it for advertising or to train AI models. No human reads Drive data except authorized team members using the application.

Storage and deletion

Encrypted copies stay in the company's Google Drive until the related record is deleted in the application, at which point the Drive copy is permanently deleted. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Compliance

Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
